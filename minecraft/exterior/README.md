# Garching exterior meshes

This is a native Minecraft Java 1.21.1 upgrade. The surrounding campus remains Google's textured 3D photogrammetry, streamed through the user's configured provider. The 0.4.0 walking-quality preview adds near the ground:

- A 600 × 600 m surveyed terrain mesh, with official DGM1 heights and DOP20 aerial imagery at 20 cm per pixel.
- Two Galileo building components with surveyed footprints/roof geometry, recessed window geometry, glass reflections and shaded metal panels.
- Three surveyed grass-roof promenade structures, including their sloping roof surfaces.
- Three reconstructed main MW components and the Graduate School, using surveyed envelopes with photo-informed façade modules, recessed glazing and projecting colour bands.
- Inferred canopy/tree instances with photographed CC0 leaf/bark atlases and reconstructed branches.
- Mipmapped CC0 grass/concrete colour and normal maps, plus atmospheric distance fading.

Elevated flight uses the photographic mesh; the detailed ground scene activates on descent, with hysteresis to prevent repeated switching near the transition. An explicit loading message remains while geometry streams.

The Galileo window pattern is reconstructed from a CC0 photograph and repeated across the surveyed walls. It is an architectural approximation. Unreconstructed façades and vegetation attached to their protected scan surfaces retain the source limits. Other noisy free-standing outdoor photo objects are retired with the old outdoor layer; they have not all been replaced with individual surveyed props. **This is a preview; the realism goal remains unfinished.** There are no reconstructed interiors in this update.

## Play

For the accumulated development improvements, use [Exterior Preview 0.5](PREVIEW-0.5.md). Its installer creates a separate launcher installation and verified copy of your existing saves. The standard installation instructions below still use 0.4.0.

Run `python3 scripts/minecraft.py install`, then launch **Minecampus — TUM Garching** from the official Minecraft Launcher. An existing provider configuration is retained. A fresh installation still needs the masked provider setup described in the [native guide](../README.md).

Create a **TUM Garching — Minecampus** world for an undamaged campus. Existing saves retain their player builds and recorded excavation/TNT damage. The deleted Mensa interior preview is no longer included.

The current source build produces a `0.5.0-dev` JAR. It includes the IAS reconstruction and survey-aligned photographic façades: 80 candidate envelopes use existing provider photography at runtime, accepting adequately covered walls and retaining unsupported source faces. Roof geometry and imagery come from the public survey. Provider pixels stay in memory and are not included in the package. Brown foliage is also recognized by the canopy detector. The normal installer remains pinned to the published 0.4.0 preview until the next release. See [IAS evidence](IAS-DEVELOPMENT.md) and [photographic façade evidence](PHOTO-FACADES-DEVELOPMENT.md).

The development renderer also uses [mapped ground surfaces](ground-surfaces.geojson): 180 lawn polygons, two paved areas and twelve water/court exclusions. The derived classification mask is regenerated by `exterior_ground_surfaces.py`; its source and resource hashes are checked during packaging. Boundaries come from OpenStreetMap contributors under [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). The selected data is included in the release service bundle, and the derived mask is supplied under the same license. Ground grain and paving joints are approximate materials, not measured individual paving units. Native evidence and remaining limitations are recorded in [the development log](PHOTO-FACADES-DEVELOPMENT.md).

The current source also adds 19 road/path strips from explicitly tagged OSM widths, clipped away from surveyed building footprints and the existing mapped surfaces. Asphalt uses a four-metre CC0 scanned material with a matching normal map; the aerial image supplies broad shading. The strips add about 7,163 m² of surface detail. Their boundaries are width estimates, and sidewalks without mapped widths remain untreated. Terrain geometry and collisions are unchanged.

UYA now has four reconstructed outer façades with recessed windows and lower panels, fitted to the BVV envelope using an ESO exterior reference. Its surveyed roof and courtyard openings are unchanged; inner courtyard walls retain photography. Exact source qualifications and native captures are in [UYA validation](uya-development-validation.json).

Telefonzentrale (TUM 5130) now has a reconstructed four-window west end on its own BVV envelope. The adjoining Siemensbau (5103) retains photography. The opening count and wall treatment follow ground references; exact sizes, recess depth and the continuation of the fascia are approximate. The survey roof and connecting passage are preserved. This source change is newer than the installed `dev.2` preview.

## How collisions and damage work

Development terrain now retains every DGM1 sample at its one-metre pixel centre. The sampler interpolates across the source-file boundary, and adjoining 100 m render tiles share identical boundary vertices. The two-metre guide remains for source-mesh filtering; rendered terrain and its voxelized collision use the finer mesh. See [terrain validation](terrain-development-validation.json) for source-representation checks and native gameplay evidence.

Both new chunks and existing nearby Garching chunks receive proxy blocks voxelized from the survey surfaces and reconstructed tree wood. Leaf cards are visual geometry, not a complete Minecraft leaf-block simulation. The migration keeps ordinary Minecraft blocks and positions in the authoritative edit journal. It suppresses recording of its own repairs, and stores completed chunks so the operation survives save/reload. Actual player mining, placement and TNT continue through Minecraft and RealWorldMC's damage system.

The collision grid remains Minecraft's one-metre block grid. A sloping photographic surface and its supporting block tops can differ by up to approximately half a block. A repeated application must preserve edits, rather than infer player damage from a changed terrain LOD.

## Rebuild

Use Python 3.9+ in an isolated environment, JDK 21, Node.js 22+ and Git:

```sh
python3 -m venv .venv-exterior
.venv-exterior/bin/pip install -r minecraft/exterior/requirements.txt
.venv-exterior/bin/python scripts/build-exterior.py --download
.venv-exterior/bin/python scripts/test_exterior_terrain.py
.venv-exterior/bin/python scripts/test_exterior_ground_extension.py
.venv-exterior/bin/python scripts/validate-terrain.py
.venv-exterior/bin/python scripts/validate-ground-surfaces.py
for site in physics-site physics-library physics-container p1-complex physics-woodland physics-new-laboratory physics-laboratory-site physics-ii pinned-water physics-wsi physics-znn riverbank courtyard-ground north-ground physics-znn-west physics-cala; do
  .venv-exterior/bin/python "scripts/validate-$site.py" || exit 1
done
python3 scripts/validate-exterior.py
python3 scripts/build-exterior-release.py --java-home /absolute/path/to/jdk-21
```

The downloader fetches only the public inputs pinned in [sources.json](sources.json). Source data is about 150 MB. Output remains in ignored `dist-minecraft/`; it contains no provider credentials or saved worlds. Official download URLs can be updated by their publisher: a changed input hash fails verification until the source manifest is deliberately updated.

The release builder checks the upstream JAR and embedded service hashes, checks out the pinned Apache-2.0 source into a generated cache directory, applies [the patch](patches/realworldmc-exterior.patch), compiles it and adds the open-data meshes to the existing portable service. It preserves the upstream runtime/licenses and adds asset notices. No Minecraft assets or Google tile files are bundled.

### LMU exterior development draft

`physics-lmu.json` and `scripts/exterior_physics_lmu.py` describe the surveyed Am Coulombwall1 complex. The generator is not yet invoked by the main build. Its source-informed facade details are qualified estimates; the current installed preview is unchanged.

For an incremental draft directory containing its manifest, photo-facade plan and mesh files, run:

```sh
.cache/geodata-venv/bin/python scripts/validate-physics-lmu.py --directory /path/to/draft
.cache/geodata-venv/bin/python scripts/test_lmu_facade_triangulation.py
```

The source validator checks encoded coverage and outward first-hit surfaces against the pinned BVV survey and DOP20 roof imagery. Native terrain support, collision, rendering and visual review remain separate required checks.
