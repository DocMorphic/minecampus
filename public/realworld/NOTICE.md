Retrieved 20 October 2026. This is an approximately 1.6 × 1.7 km campus subset with additional edge tiles, not the entire regional archive. Modifications: I3S geometry converted into a local east/up/south coordinate frame, indexed and quantized; JPEG texture atlases re-encoded to WebP. The manifest contains the tile bounds, source, and attribution.

These are real aerial-derived exterior surfaces, including trees, terrain, roofs, and infrastructure. The original source is a 2.5D surface mesh with stretched/blurry facade textures and vegetation artefacts. It does not include surveyed indoor rooms or underground geometry. Interior linings revealed by in-game blasts are simulated game assets, not part of the survey. Gameplay can alter the displayed geometry; changes are local and do not modify the source archive.
